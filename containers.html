Rental and Modification Services

Beyond container sales, we support temporary and custom-use requirements.

Flexible Rentals

Weekly, monthly and longer-term rental arrangements for temporary storage, projects and seasonal needs. Delivery, placement and pickup are coordinated. Optional lockboxes, shelving and basic modifications can be discussed.

Custom Modifications

Options include additional doors and windows, insulation, interior lining, electrical packages and lighting, shelving, partitions, workbenches, security upgrades, ventilation, flooring and office or workshop fit-outs.

Flat-Rack Containers

Open-sided containers with fixed or collapsible end frames for oversized, heavy or irregular cargo.

Common applications

  • Machinery and industrial equipment
  • Pipes, steel and project cargo

  • Strong base structure

Open-Top Containers

Containers with a removable roof arrangement, typically covered by tarpaulin, for overhead loading.

Common applications

  • Tall machinery and industrial equipment
  • Bulk materials loaded by crane or conveyor
  • Cargo that cannot pass through standard doors

Key features

  • Removable roof or tarpaulin arrangement
  • Standard container footprint and corner castings
  • Configuration confirmed for the selected unit
