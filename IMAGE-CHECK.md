# Website image asset check

The original company logo and its cropped wide display derivative are included under `assets/images/brand/`. The three company-approved team photographs are included under `assets/images/team/`. The company-supplied hero image and local illustrative port, depot, container, vessel and transport images are included in the same archive.

All local image files were decoded successfully during this project check. Product, port, warehouse, vessel and logistics photographs are illustrative unless expressly identified otherwise. The stock-photo provenance and commercial-use licence for the legacy illustrative files were not supplied with the archive and must be confirmed or replaced before commercial publication.

Preserve the complete `assets/images/` directory structure when uploading. Upload the contents of the project folder to the hosting document root; do not upload only the HTML files.
