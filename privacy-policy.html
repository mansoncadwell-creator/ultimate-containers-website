Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, store and protect information shared through this website.

1. Who this policy covers

This notice explains how Ultimate Containers Inc. handles personal information submitted through this website, including quotation enquiries, general messages and customer reviews.

2. Information we may collect

When you contact us, we may collect your name, business contact details, container requirements, delivery location, purchasing timeframe and any information you choose to include in your message. Basic technical information such as browser type, server logs and security events may also be processed by the hosting provider.

Customer reviews

If you submit a review, we collect the name, email address, rating and review text you provide. Your email address is kept private. We require consent to process the submission for moderation. Public display is a separate, optional choice; a review will not be published unless you grant that permission and an administrator approves it.

3. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to enquiries, prepare quotations, assess supply and delivery options, communicate about a requested transaction, protect the website from abuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. Do not submit payment card details or other sensitive personal information through this website.

4. Sharing and service providers

Information may be shared with service providers where reasonably necessary to host the website, deliver communications, assess a quotation or coordinate a requested container or delivery service. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

5. Retention and security

We retain enquiry and review information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to the request, maintain business records and meet legal requirements. We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, although no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable legal and recordkeeping requirements.

7. Cookies and analytics

Essential session and technical storage may be used for secure forms and basic website operation. The cookie preference control stores your choice in your browser. Optional analytics are off by default and no analytics provider or advertising tracker is currently configured. If optional analytics are introduced later, the policy and consent controls will be updated before activation.

8. International processing

Because the Company serves customers in more than one country and may use hosting or service providers in different locations, information may be processed in the country where those providers operate. We take reasonable steps to use service providers appropriate to the requested service and applicable legal requirements.

9. Changes and contact

We may update this notice when our website practices or legal obligations change. For privacy questions or requests, contact ultimatecontainersinc@gmail.com.
