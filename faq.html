Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers about our containers, process, and international service.

We supply new shipping containers, used shipping containers, refrigerated (reefer) containers, modified containers, flat-rack containers, and open-top containers. The range is designed to cover common commercial, storage, and specialised cargo needs.
Yes. We offer both new and used containers. New units are factory-fresh. Used units are inspected for structural soundness and condition. You can indicate a preference for new, used, or either when you request a quotation.
Yes. Refrigerated containers (reefers) are part of our range. They are used for temperature-controlled transport and storage of perishable and temperature-sensitive goods. Availability and specifications are confirmed when you enquire.
Common sizes for standard dry containers include 20 ft and 40 ft units. Other sizes and specialised configurations may be available depending on inventory and manufacturer supply. Exact sizes and availability are confirmed as part of the quotation process.
Yes. We handle both single-container and multi-container enquiries. Please indicate the quantity required on the quotation form so we can match availability and logistics accordingly.
Our primary service area is the United States, Canada and Mexico. Selected international projects may be supported through our partner network. Delivery arrangements depend on the container type, destination, availability and applicable logistics requirements.
Yes. We coordinate shipping and delivery using our own assets (warehouses, depots, vessels, trucks) and, where necessary, partner services. The approach is tailored to the destination and the specific container. Details are discussed during the quotation stage.
Use the quotation enquiry form on our Contact page. Provide your name, email, container type, preferred size, quantity, condition preference, delivery city and country, and any additional information. You may also contact us by email or telephone using the details on the Contact page.
After we receive your enquiry, our team reviews current inventory and supply arrangements. We then confirm which options match your requirements and prepare a quotation based on that information. Availability can change, so confirmation is always part of the individual enquiry process.
Delivery time depends on the container type, current availability, the delivery destination, and the logistics method used. We do not publish fixed delivery periods on the website. Estimated timeframes are provided during the quotation process once your specific requirements and destination are known.
