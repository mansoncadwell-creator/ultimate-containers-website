Returns and Cancellation Policy

Order changes, cancellations, returns and remedies are handled according to the accepted written agreement and applicable law.

1. General position

Container sales are quotation-based and may involve allocation, inspection, preparation, customization, procurement and transport arrangements. A request for a return or cancellation is not automatically accepted by submitting a website enquiry.

2. Before acceptance

If you wish to change or withdraw an enquiry, contact the Company promptly. An enquiry alone does not constitute an accepted order and no payment is collected through this website.

3. Deposit refunds before dispatch

Deposits paid to Ultimate Containers Inc. are refundable when the customer cancels before the container is dispatched. Contact the Company promptly so the order can be stopped and the refund arranged. The Company will confirm the refund method and processing details in writing. This policy does not limit any additional rights that apply under law.

4. After dispatch

Once a container has been dispatched, cancellation, return arrangements and any transport or other costs are handled under the accepted written order and applicable law. Any proposed deduction or charge must be explained to the customer and supported by the order terms and applicable law; no unsupported penalty is implied by this policy.

5. Returns and condition concerns

If a delivered container appears damaged, materially different from the agreed specification or incomplete, record the issue on delivery documentation where possible, take photographs and notify the Company promptly. Do not modify, repair, relocate or dispose of the unit before receiving instructions, except where necessary for immediate safety.

6. Customized or specially procured units

Units that are modified, specially configured, procured for a specific order or shipped internationally may have additional restrictions or non-recoverable costs. Any such conditions must be disclosed in the written quotation or contract before acceptance.

7. Other refunds

For matters other than a customer cancellation before dispatch, refund eligibility and remedies are assessed under the accepted written agreement and applicable law. Contact our team before arranging a return.

8. Statutory rights

Nothing in this policy is intended to remove rights that cannot lawfully be excluded under applicable consumer or commercial law.

9. How to request assistance

Contact us at ultimatecontainersinc@gmail.com or +1 326-202-0106. Include your order reference, container details, delivery date and supporting photographs so our team can review the request.
