How We Work

A clear, practical process from first contact to delivery of your containers.

Customer Process

Eight Straightforward Steps

We keep the path from enquiry to delivery simple so international and domestic customers can understand what to expect.

1. Contact Us

You reach Ultimate Containers Inc. by form, email, or telephone with your initial enquiry.

2. Explain Requirements

You tell us the container type, preferred size, quantity, condition preference (new, used, or either), and the intended delivery city and country.

3. Identify Options

Our team reviews available inventory and supply arrangements to identify containers that match your requirements.

4. Confirm Availability

We confirm specifications, condition, and availability for the units under consideration.

5. Provide Quotation

A clear quotation is prepared covering the containers and, where applicable, the logistics options for delivery.

6. Complete Purchase

Purchase arrangements are completed directly with Ultimate Containers Inc. according to the agreed terms.

7. Coordinate Shipping

Shipping and delivery are planned and coordinated using company assets and, where needed, partner services.

8. Receive Container

The container is delivered to the agreed destination in line with the confirmed logistics plan.

Delivery Arrangements

Delivery depends on several practical factors:

  • The type and condition of the container
  • The delivery city and country
  • Current availability of suitable units
  • Applicable logistics and regulatory requirements

We coordinate container sourcing and delivery through the appropriate operating resources and logistics partners. The arrangements for each order are confirmed in the quotation.

We do not provide guaranteed delivery periods on the website. Timeframes are discussed and confirmed during the quotation stage once destination and container details are known.

Transport Coordination

Movement Planned Around the Destination

Where inland transport is part of the agreed scope, we assess the destination, site access and handling requirements and coordinate suitable equipment through company operations or logistics partners as applicable.

Ready to Start?

The most helpful first step is to send us your requirements using the quotation form. Include as much detail as you can about type, size, quantity, condition preference, and delivery location.
