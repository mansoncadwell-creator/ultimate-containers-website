# Image attribution, licensing and provenance

## Provenance status

The supplied archive included an earlier attribution note stating that its photographs were sourced from Unsplash. However, the archive did not include individual photo-page URLs, photographer names, download dates or a file-to-source mapping. The JPEG files contain no usable photographer/source metadata. I cannot independently verify that assertion or confirm a commercial-use licence for any individual image, and will not invent attribution records.

The build environment could not retrieve external image binaries for local integration. I therefore kept the supplied local photographs rather than adding external hotlinks or pretending that an image had been downloaded. The original source and licence must be confirmed before public launch. If the original photo records cannot be recovered, replace the relevant images with genuine company photography or newly downloaded photographs whose individual source page and licence have been checked.

## Product-image accuracy

The current category cards use distinct local images showing shipping-container yards, ports, vessels and loading operations. The archive did not contain verified photographs for each specialized product type. In particular, current visuals should not be interpreted as exact depictions of reefer, modified, flat-rack or open-top units. Page alt text and nearby notices describe them as illustrative. Replace them with product-accurate imagery before marketing individual models.

Stock images do not depict or prove ownership of Ultimate Containers Inc. facilities, vessels, trucks or inventory.
