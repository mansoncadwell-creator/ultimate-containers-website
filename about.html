About Ultimate Containers Inc.

Container solutions shaped by experience, clear communication and customer needs.

Established 2012

Practical Container Solutions Since 2012

Ultimate Containers Inc. provides shipping container sales, rentals and modifications for commercial, industrial, storage and project requirements.

Our work includes sourcing new one-trip and factory-new containers, inspected used units, specialist equipment and custom modifications. We provide inspection and certification processes and coordinate delivery according to the destination and agreed scope.

The company is registered in both the United States and Canada. Our primary service area is the United States, Canada and Mexico. Selected international projects may be supported through our partner network.
